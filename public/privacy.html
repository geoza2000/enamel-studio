Privacy in Enamel Studio

Your badge designs and imported files are processed in your browser. We do not upload your artwork, design names, or downloaded files.

Optional Google Analytics

If you choose “Allow analytics”, Google Analytics measures page visits and associated browser, device, session, and approximate-location information. It uses first-party cookies to distinguish visits. Google receives ordinary connection information, including your IP address, when its service is contacted. Advertising storage, advertising personalization, and Google Signals are disabled in this app.

Google’s analytics tag is not loaded until you opt in. We exclude page query strings, URL fragments, and referring URLs from the page-view information configured by this app. Your choice is stored locally in this browser.

Use “Privacy settings” in the editor and choose “No thanks” to withdraw consent. This disables future analytics collection and removes accessible Google Analytics cookies for this site. It does not erase information already received by Google. Blocking analytics does not affect badge editing or downloads.

Firebase Hosting serves the site and can process normal hosting request logs independently of analytics.

For details about Google’s processing, see Google’s Privacy Policy.
